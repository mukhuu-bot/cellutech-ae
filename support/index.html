Platforms covered

What to include

To help us investigate efficiently, include the application name and version, your device and operating system, a clear description of the issue, the time it occurred and any relevant screenshots. Do not email passwords, verification codes, full medical records, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Institution-managed accounts

If your account was provided by a hospital, pharmacy, school, government body, employer or other institution, contact that institution’s administrator first for access, record corrections and organization-specific workflows. CelluTech may need the institution’s authorization before changing an account or data.

Privacy and security

For a privacy request, use the subject “Privacy request.” To report a suspected vulnerability or security incident, use the subject “Security report” and provide enough detail for investigation without including exploit data or sensitive records in the first message.
